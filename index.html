The Mayapur Diaries

A Family Pilgrimage

A three-day spiritual immersion into the heart of Bengal. Six family members, two generations, and a journey from the bustling streets of Goalpara to the ancient, sacred riverbanks of the Ganges.

Swipe to Begin ⬆️

The Great Iron Road

The transition from the material world begins on the rails. As the afternoon fades, the Saraighat Express carries the family out of Assam, making way for a high-speed, overnight dash across Bengal aboard the Vande Bharat.

1. Saraighat Exp (12346): Dep 14:40 GLPT ➔ Arr 17:30 NCB
2. Vande Bharat (27576): Dep 21:45 NCB ➔ Arr 06:13 Nabadwip
Swipe Up ⬆️

Nabadwip Dham

Arriving at 06:13 AM, you step onto the soil of Nabadwip—the "City of Nine Islands." Founded by the Sena dynasty in the 11th century, this ancient center of learning was the Oxford of Bengal. It is the birthplace of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu (born 1486), the great saint who sparked the Bhakti movement that would eventually sweep across the globe.

Swipe Up ⬆️

Hulor Ghat: The Crossing

To reach Mayapur, one must cross the mighty Ganges (Bhagirathi) River. The wooden ferry ride from Nabadwip to Hulor Ghat is not just a transit point; it is a symbolic crossing. The cool morning breeze and the sound of the boat's motor mark the final shedding of city stress before entering the sanctuary of the ISKCON campus.

Ishodyan Bhavan

Your sanctuary for the next two days. Named after the spiritual gardens of the Vedic texts, this guesthouse provides a quiet retreat for the elders. It sits within walking distance of the main shrines, offering a grounding space amidst the immense energy of the surrounding temples.

A Modern Marvel: TOVP

The Temple of the Vedic Planetarium (TOVP) towers over the Bengal skyline. Decades in the making, its massive domes combine traditional Vedic architecture with modern engineering. Inside, it houses a gigantic, functioning cosmological chandelier, designed to map the universe exactly as it is described in the ancient Srimad Bhagavatam.

Swipe Up ⬆️

The Spiritual Heart

As evening falls, the massive Sri Mayapur Chandrodaya Mandir comes alive. The Sandhya Aarti (evening worship) creates an atmosphere of pure, electric devotion. Nearby, set amidst immaculate gardens, stands the Pushpa Samadhi of Srila Prabhupada—a breathtaking marble mausoleum honoring the man who brought these teachings to the Western world at the age of 69.

Swipe Up ⬆️

The Goshala & Jalangi River

Beyond the marble temples lies the quiet soul of Mayapur. The pristine ISKCON Goshala is a sprawling sanctuary dedicated to cow protection, offering a serene, pastoral escape. Just a short walk away flow the waters of the Jalangi River, offering the perfect, peaceful morning walk before the journey home.
